RaanuBot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

RaanuBot is a private dashboard for monitoring a single Alpaca paper-trading account. It is not a public service and is not offered to third parties.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app collects no personal information, creates no account, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software of any kind. There is no third-party SDK in the application.

What the app displays

The app is a viewer for one private server operated by the app's owner. It displays trading information — portfolio value, positions, order history and strategy signals — that is fetched from that server, which in turn reads it from the owner's own brokerage account at Alpaca. That information is shown on the device and is not stored by the app, beyond the browser's normal temporary cache.

What is stored on your device

  • An access passphrase, saved in local storage so the app does not ask for it on every launch. It never leaves the device except as an authorisation header to the owner's own server.
  • A cached copy of the app's interface (HTML and icons), so the app opens without a network connection. Account figures are deliberately not cached.

Both are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

What is shared

No data is shared with anyone. No data is sold, transferred, or disclosed to third parties. Network traffic goes only between your device and the owner's server over HTTPS.

Who can use it

Access requires a passphrase issued by the owner. The app is distributed privately to a small number of named testers and is not intended for, or directed at, the general public or children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. To have the underlying trading records removed from the server, contact the owner at the address below.
